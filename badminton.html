Badminton

What is Badminton?

Badminton is one of the most popular indoor sports in the world. It is played with rackets and a shuttlecock. It requires speed, agility and focus.

History

Badminton was invented in British India. It was first played in Pune, Maharashtra, hence it was earlier called "Poona". Later British officers took it to England.

Rules

  • Match is of 21 points, 3 sets.
  • Can be played as singles or doubles.
  • Shuttlecock should not touch the ground.
